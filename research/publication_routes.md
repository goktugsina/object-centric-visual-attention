## Recommended sequence

Prepare the existing bounded empirical study as an arXiv preprint, obtain critical feedback, and then decide whether to adapt it for a peer-reviewed journal. Do not expand the architecture merely to postpone submission. Conversely, a complete draft and passing software checks do not establish scientific novelty or guarantee acceptance.

## What Perception would require

The current IEEE-formatted draft has not been converted to the journal's presentation requirements. A standard article is the initial format to investigate. Its short-report route has a 4,000-word total limit and a 150-word abstract limit; the current 170-word abstract would need shortening for that route. The journal also expects a contribution meaningful to perceptual science, not merely an engineering performance table. Emphasize what the annotation-versus-detection comparison and transfer failures reveal, while avoiding causal or neural-mechanism claims.

The current guidelines accept preprints and require authors to disclose the preprint identifier on submission. They also state that the preprint should not be changed while the manuscript is under journal evaluation. Any eventual journal submission needs a fresh requirements check. No fee waiver or institutional publishing agreement is assumed.

Journal of Eye Movement Research was also investigated because its subject matter is closely related. The older Bern portal says it is not accepting submissions, while publisher records point to MDPI. The current MDPI scope and fee pages could not be retrieved reliably during this check. Accordingly, neither the archived EUR 870 fee nor the old submission portal is treated as current guidance or an actionable recommendation. See [archived portal](https://bop.unibe.ch/JEMR/about/submissions) and [publisher imprint](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/jemr/imprint).
